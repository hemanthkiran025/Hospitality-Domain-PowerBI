End-to-end Power BI project on the Hospitality Domain

# 🏨 Hospitality Domain - Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes hospitality revenue data across 4 Indian cities (Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Delhi) covering May–July 2022. Key findings include Mumbai's market leadership, high weekend demand, and a critical 25% cancellation rate that presents a major revenue recovery opportunity.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power Query

## 📊 Key Insights
💰 Revenue Performance

Total revenue stands at ₹1.69 Billion across all properties and cities
Business category dominates with ~58% revenue share vs Luxury at ~42%
RevPAR is ₹7,337 and ADR is ₹12,696 overall

🏨 Occupancy & Realisation

Overall occupancy is 57.79% — indicating roughly 4 in 10 rooms go unsold, a clear improvement opportunity
Weekends perform better (62.64% occupancy) vs Weekdays (55.85%) — guests prefer leisure stays
Realisation rate is healthy at 80.18% across all platforms

🏙️ City-wise Performance

Mumbai leads with highest bookings (43K) and revenue (₹0.67bn)
Hyderabad comes second with 35K bookings and ₹0.33bn revenue
Delhi has the lowest bookings (24K) despite having top-rated properties

📅 Booking Patterns

"Others" platform dominates with 40.91% of bookings
MakeYourTrip is the leading named OTA platform at ~20%
Direct online bookings are the lowest — suggesting a need to invest in direct channel marketing

❌ Cancellation Concerns

Total cancellations: 33K out of 135K bookings (~24.84%) — quite high
No-shows account for 6,759 bookings — lost revenue with no recovery
Cancellation rate is consistent across properties (~24-25%), suggesting a policy-level issue rather than property-specific

🏆 Top Properties

Atliq Exotica Mumbai generated the highest revenue at ₹93M
Atliq Palace Mumbai has the best occupancy at 66.13% with ₹100M revenue
Atliq Blu Delhi holds the highest average rating of 4.36
